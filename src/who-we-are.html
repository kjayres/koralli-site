03 / WHY KORALLI

A reef grows
through relationships.

koralli takes its name from κοράλλι, Greek for coral.

A reef grows through relationships between different organisms. That is the idea behind our name: people with distinct specialisms working closely enough to build something together, while retaining what each brings.
