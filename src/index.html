WORKING WITH A GLOBAL BANK

A clearer picture of delivery.

A global bank needed a dependable view of how its technology projects were performing. Its records used different definitions, making delivery difficult to compare.

Working alongside the bank, we agreed useful measures, reconciled the records and built dashboards that connected portfolio performance to the projects behind it.
