03 / THE ORGANISATION

We start with the organisation.

Introducing AI changes more than the tools people use.

It raises questions about who does the work, who makes decisions and who is accountable.

We help organisations work through those questions, from their operating model to the data underneath it. That includes deciding where agents can take on more of the work, where human judgement should remain central, and how the two work together.

WHAT WE WORK ON

  • Measuring delivery performance

    Agree useful measures and build a dependable view of delivery.

  • Governing AI agents

    Set responsibilities, evaluations and controls for agents in use.

  • Making data ready for people and agents

    Resolve inconsistent records, definitions and ownership.

  • Redesigning work for people and agents

    Rethink tasks, handovers and decisions, then test the new arrangement.
