KORALLI WORKSPACE

IN DEVELOPMENT

A reef of specialists.

A workspace for coordinating agents with different roles, shared sources and people responsible for the decisions.

Like a reef, the idea depends on different contributions working together. Research, analysis and review each need their own perspective. We are exploring how to make those relationships visible, so people can follow the work and decide what happens next.

THE THINKING BEHIND IT ↓
THE DESIGN PRINCIPLES

Different roles. A shared question.

01

Specialist roles.

Give each agent a defined task, useful tools and a clear scope. The aim is to combine different strengths, with responsibilities that people can understand and change.

02

Shared sources.

Keep the records and definitions close to the work. Findings should lead back to evidence, with gaps and conflicting accounts available for the team to examine.

03

Human judgement.

Make review part of the workflow. People should be able to inspect a result, question its reasoning, request changes and decide which actions to take.

WHERE THE RESEARCH GOES NEXT

Which combinations are useful?

One direction is to compare different open models in complementary roles, then evaluate whether their coordination improves the work. That means looking at evidence quality, reliability, cost and the effort required from the people using them.

The prototype lets us explore the interface first. Those questions about model behaviour and performance remain research work.
