AN ANONYMISED BANK ENGAGEMENT

How that worked with a global bank

The ambition was clear. How to measure it wasn’t.

A Tier-1 global bank wanted to understand the performance of a project portfolio representing more than $100 million in annual investment.

We worked alongside the bank in two-week sprints, bringing together stakeholder research, operational experience, data analysis and rapid development to move progressively from question to working capability.

Sprints 1–2 Phase 1 — Go & See +

Before building, we needed to understand the problem. We worked with stakeholders across the organisation to explore how portfolio performance should be understood, which measures would be meaningful, and what data could support them.

Sprints 3–4 Phase 2 — Build & Learn +

Working with the bank’s data, we built an initial set of dashboards around the key performance measures, with the ability to move from a portfolio-wide view into the underlying detail. Putting prototypes in front of users created a faster learning loop: build, use, challenge, learn, refine.

Sprints 5–6 Phase 3 — Adapt & Grow +

The goal was to leave behind a capability that continues to develop. We deployed the dashboards throughout the bank’s reporting environment and used the new tool to analyse portfolio performance. We began exploring what the data could reveal beyond reporting — using historical patterns to look forward, not just backwards.

Our service offerings.

Each engagement is shaped around the question in front of you, but the work tends to fall into four areas.

Find where AI genuinely changes the work, then build it in with the people who will rely on it. Practical use cases, tested in the real environment, not a strategy deck.

Operating Model

Design how people and AI work together: who decides, who acts and where human judgement stays essential. A model built around the work, not the org chart.

Governance that keeps pace with the technology. Clear ownership, continuous evaluation and accountability that hold up in production, not just on paper.

Research & Analysis
