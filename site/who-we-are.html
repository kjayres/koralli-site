Our interdisciplinary teams

Drawn from two complementary groups: Researchers from the University of Oxford + Senior Operators.

We embrace multiple viewpoints and value our differences. We do not homogenise our different backgrounds into teams of like-minded robots.

We are aware of the shortfalls in the consulting industry and we aspire to challenge the status quo.

Our post-graduates are on the cutting edge of research, exploring some of the most complex and challenging problems of our time. Our experienced professionals know what works and what doesn’t work, ensuring solutions are fit for purpose.

Our network provides access to leading research and industry experience.
