CASE 01 · FINANCIAL SERVICES · ANONYMISED 8 WEEKS

“Can we deploy language models in claims handling before the rules settle?”

TEAM
ML researcher, Oxford Regulation & policy researcher Former insurance COO
EVIDENCE

Evaluation of six models against real claims transcripts; obligation mapping under incoming AI regulation; failure-mode review with the claims team.

INTERVENTION

A governed pilot in one claims line — human sign-off thresholds, escalation rules and a full audit trail.

RESULT

31% faster cycle time in the pilot line. The governance pattern was adopted group-wide.

CASE 02 · PROFESSIONAL SERVICES · ANONYMISED 12 WEEKS

“Which of our specialist services will AI commoditise first — and what do we build instead?”

TEAM
Economics researcher, Oxford Former services-firm partner Product lead
EVIDENCE

Task-level analysis of three service lines; pricing and win-rate data; fourteen client interviews.

INTERVENTION

Re-priced two offers and prototyped an AI-assisted delivery model with the incumbent team.

RESULT

New offer in market in twelve weeks; margin held on renegotiated contracts.
